# Fındık Universe — Product Vision

## North Star
A beloved sticker becomes a tiny living digital world that feels personal every time it is opened.

## Experience promise
The user should never feel like they opened a website. They should feel like they visited Fındık.

## Core loop
1. Open the app and meet Fındık in a time-aware home scene.
2. Touch Fındık for a reaction or choose an adventure.
3. Complete a short playful story beat.
4. Receive a memory card, XP and possibly an achievement/sticker.
5. Return later and see persistent progress.

## V1 world
Home connects to five places: Tram Stop, Bicycle Path, Café, Park and Night Garden. Each location contains one authored adventure with its own reward.

## UX direction
- iPhone portrait is source of truth.
- Warm tactile storybook atmosphere; premium but playful.
- Large character, minimal chrome, thumb-friendly controls.
- Micro-interactions instead of dense UI.
- No ads, payments, streak pressure or manipulative retention.

## Future ceiling
V2 may add wardrobe, expanded map, seasonal scenes and richer character animation. V3 may add a constrained AI Adventure Studio that creates new adventures while preserving the Character Bible.
