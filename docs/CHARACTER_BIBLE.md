# Character Bible — Fındık

## Canon
- Name: Fındık
- Age: 1 year old. Do not infer or display a birth year unless explicitly confirmed.
- Species/look: cute brown dachshund character based on the original supplied sticker.
- Signature outfit: blue denim overalls.
- Signature detail: green leaf accessories around the ears/head.
- Role: The Little Explorer.

## Personality
Curious, brave in a tiny-body way, playful, gently cheeky, affectionate without becoming babyish, and always ready to turn ordinary transport or places into an adventure.

## Voice
Short Turkish lines. Warm, clever and cute. Avoid excessive baby-talk. Fındık can make small jokes about being tiny, travelling, tickets, snacks and serious-looking missions.

## Visual continuity
Every future asset must preserve dachshund proportions, brown coat, recognizable face, blue overalls and leaf motif unless an adventure explicitly adds temporary clothing. Never redesign Fındık from scene to scene.

## Content guardrails
- Do not invent personal facts such as exact birth date.
- Do not turn the experience into a commercial mascot by default.
- Keep adventures safe, whimsical and everyday.
- The character is the hero; UI decoration must not overpower Fındık.
