# Context — Fındık Universe

## Why this exists
Fındık Universe begins with a loved sticker character, not a market gap. Its first job is to give its owner a warm, surprising, revisitable place where Fındık feels alive.

This is personal-first. It may later become a polished public product, but growth must not erase the intimacy, humour or recognisable identity that makes it valuable.

## The person and the moment
The primary experience is a short visit on an iPhone: open the app, meet Fındık, do one small thing, leave with a smile or memory. It should feel closer to visiting a tiny friend than browsing a site.

## Product thesis
A persistent character world earns revisits through authored care:
- Fındık changes gently with time and place.
- Ordinary errands become tiny missions.
- Finished moments remain as memories.
- Progress celebrates exploration, never absence.

## Non-goals
- A generic AI pet generator
- An ad-funded children’s attention machine
- A complicated RPG or social network
- A mascot redesign exercise
- A promise of infinite generated content before the authored core is excellent

## Success definition
A successful first release is one the intended owner installs, opens more than once by choice, recognises immediately as Fındık, and wants to show someone else.

## Language and tone
Turkish is the canonical product language. English labels may exist only where they are brand-signature or technically necessary. Copy is warm, compact, clever and never patronising.
