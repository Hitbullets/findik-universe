# Decisions Log

## 2026-08-10 — Personal-first product boundary
Fındık Universe is designed first for its intended owner. Commercialisation, accounts and monetisation are explicitly deferred.

## 2026-08-10 — Authored core before AI
Five strong authored adventures and a canonical asset set come before AI-generated adventures. This protects character quality and gives future AI a reliable structure.

## 2026-08-10 — Local-first progress
Progress remains device-local during the first production milestone. Cloud backup requires an explicit later decision.

## 2026-08-10 — iPhone PWA is the source experience
Portrait iPhone is the primary quality bar. Desktop can be supported but must not drive visual or interaction decisions.

## 2026-08-10 — Handmade visual standard
Warm storybook craft, intentional scenes and restrained motion are required. Generic AI visual language is not acceptable.
