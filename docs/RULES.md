# Rules — Fındık Universe

## 1. Canon rules
- Fındık is a cute brown dachshund, age 1, with blue denim overalls and green leaf accessories.
- Do not invent personal facts, dates, relationships or backstory.
- Temporary clothing may change; silhouette, face, coat, overalls and leaf motif must stay recognisable.
- The Character Bible wins if any document conflicts.

## 2. Experience rules
- Every feature must improve meeting Fındık, exploring, remembering or expressing affection.
- No ads, payments, streak loss, fear of missing out, loot boxes or timed pressure.
- Replays are welcome; canonical memories are created once per adventure.
- The app must remain joyful after a week away.

## 3. Content rules
- Turkish first; short, playful, observant writing.
- Adventure stakes stay small, safe and whimsical.
- Do not use humiliation, danger, frightening imagery or manipulative emotion.
- Ordinary places are allowed to feel hilariously important.

## 4. Privacy rules
- Local-only progress is the default.
- An account is optional and only introduced when backup has genuine value.
- Never collect more personal data than a feature needs.
- Sharing must be explicit and previewed before leaving the app.

## 5. AI rules
- AI is never allowed to silently rewrite Fındık’s canon.
- Generated adventures start as drafts, not automatic publication.
- AI cannot generate private facts about the owner.
- Production visual generation requires a consistency and safety review.
- When AI is unavailable, authored play remains fully usable.

## 6. Delivery rules
- Do not claim PWA, offline, iPhone or deployment success without testing it.
- Do not replace production assets with a placeholder without marking it.
- A feature needs a success state, a repeat state and an empty/error state.
